      Field manual

      How to care for your pet

      Your pet keeps living while you’re away. Visit it from time to time.

      1. 01

        Keep it fed

        Fullness falls over time. Below 20, your pet starts losing Health. At 80 or more, Health slowly recovers.

      2. 02

        Keep it happy

        Petting improves Mood a little. Playing gives a bigger boost, but also makes your pet hungry. Play unlocks after your pet hatches.

      3. 03

        Check the world

        Weather in your pet’s city and changes in the ETH market can affect its Mood. Freezing weather can also lower Health.

      4. 04

        Watch it grow

        Your pet grows from Egg to Hatchling, Kitten, Adult, and Elder. How you care for it slowly shapes its Personality. Visits unlock at the Kitten stage.

      5. 05

        Care together

        Anyone can feed a public pet. If a pet runs out of Health, it can be revived for 1 GEN. It may use GEN collected from meals to revive itself.

      Viewing is free. Actions require wallet confirmation.
